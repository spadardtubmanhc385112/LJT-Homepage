## Personal Details

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/Vicent0205), [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://github.com/Vicent0205) (HKUST), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://github.com/Vicent0205). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://github.com/Vicent0205) (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.
